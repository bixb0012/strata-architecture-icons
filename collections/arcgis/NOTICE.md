## Changes from the original

This collection redraws all 122 icons and 10 containers and labels from Esri's toolkit. The changes are:

1. **Drawing style.** Esri's outline icons are redrawn as filled, layered shapes. Where one object covers another, the covered object is drawn whole, with a gap around the one in front, instead of being cut away.
2. **Colour.** A palette per category replaces Esri's single line colour per stencil:
   - Servers are blue, data stores violet, apps red-orange, general items teal, IT charcoal and people green.
   - Role badges are amber, and variant markers are gold.
   - The globe and cloud keep natural colours.
3. **Themes.** Each icon comes in a light and a dark version.
4. **Consistency.** Line weights, sizes and positions are the same across the set. Racks, cylinders and people match from icon to icon, and a few irregular shapes are evened out.
5. **Distinctions.**
   - Icons that are identical drawings in Esri's set carry variant chips: ETL, Python Script, Site, ArcGIS Enterprise, and the File, Mobile and Enterprise geodatabases.
   - The vendor geodatabases also carry the enterprise chip.
   - The Azure file share tags use Microsoft's product names.
6. **Containers and labels.** These are restyled in a neutral grey, with dark-theme versions.
7. **Formats.** The collection adds SVG and PNG files. The Visio stencils are Esri's stencils with each shape's artwork replaced. Esri's shape names, connection points and caption positions are kept.
8. **Alternate sets.** Variant E and Variant EM offer versions of some icons that stay closer to Esri's originals.
